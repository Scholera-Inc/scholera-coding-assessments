## Before you start

**Read the whole assignment first.** Each one tells you what matters and what we're not scoring.
Several of them explicitly ask for *less* — one thing finished beats five things started, and
that is not a figure of speech.

**Everyone records a video.** Whatever role you applied for, we ask for a 5–8 minute video of you
presenting your project. Each assignment says what to cover. It's worth taking seriously.

**You can use AI coding tools.** We use them every day and it would be strange if you didn't.
Every assignment asks you to keep a short note on where they helped and where they led you
wrong, and that note is read carefully. In the review call you'll need to explain your own work.

**Some ambiguity is deliberate.** Where an assignment doesn't tell you how to do something, that
is usually the part we're most interested in. Make a decision, write down why, and move on.

**If something is broken or you're blocked on setup, get in touch** — reply to the email that
sent you this assignment, or write to **proscio@scholera-inc.com**. Environment problems are our
fault, not a test, and they shouldn't cost you any of your time budget.
