| Role | Assignment | Effort |
|---|---|---|

## Before you start

**Read the whole assignment first.** Each one tells you what matters and what we're not scoring.
Both of them explicitly ask for *less* — one thing finished beats five things started, and that
is not a figure of speech.

**You can use AI coding tools.** We use them every day and it would be strange if you didn't.
Both assignments ask you to keep a short note on where they helped and where they led you wrong,
and that note is read carefully. In the review call you'll need to explain your own code.

**Some ambiguity is deliberate.** Where an assignment doesn't tell you how to do something, that
is usually the part we're most interested in. Make a decision, write down why, and move on.

**If something is broken or you're blocked on setup, get in touch** — reply to the email that
sent you this assignment, or write to **proscio@scholera-inc.com**. Environment problems are
our fault, not a test, and they shouldn't cost you any of your time budget.
